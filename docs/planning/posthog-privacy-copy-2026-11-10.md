BASIC corrected the behavior on November 11: Off stops future collection without deleting history. Explicit deletion requests go through the existing Privacy request form and operator verification. The revised draft sentence is: "Turning analytics Off stops new optional collection. It does not delete previously collected analytics." No promise of legal anonymity or blanket compliance is made.
